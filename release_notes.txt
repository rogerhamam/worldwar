World War 2.16.0 - Multiplayer, and an AI that actually advances

Two releases in one: 2.15.0 was prepared but never went out, so everything
from it ships here as well.

MULTIPLAYER IS PLAYABLE FROM THE MENU
- Main Menu now splits into Single Player and Multiplayer.
- Multiplayer: host a game or join one by address. The host is shown every
  address to read out, and the game asks your router to open the port for you
  (UPnP) in the background - a LAN or VPN game needs none of that and works
  regardless.
- A proper lobby: both players pick their own civilisation, leader, colour and
  team; the host sets the map and the rules; both mark themselves ready, and
  the host starts. It is the same setup screen as single player.
- Under it: deterministic lockstep with per-turn desync detection, so both
  machines simulate the identical match from the identical inputs.

  IMPORTANT: this bumps the network protocol. You can only play multiplayer
  with people who have also updated - older builds are refused at the
  handshake rather than silently desyncing ten minutes in.

THE AI NOW REACHES THE LATER AGES
- Fixed the reason the AI sat in the Industrial era all game with a huge score
  and never advanced. It refused to protect the food for its next age until it
  had already saved 60% of the cost - which it never could, because its
  barracks spent every spare scrap the moment it was gathered. It now reserves
  the age cost as soon as the age is legal.
  Across 20 measured team-instances: reaching the War era went from 0% to 15%,
  the Scientific era from 0% to 5%, and idle town-centre time fell from 777
  seconds a match to 228.
- Production buildings no longer stack deep queues. The AI re-checks every
  building constantly, so a long queue never made it build faster - it only
  froze resources it had already paid for, including the resources it needed
  to age up.

NEW DIFFICULTY: HARDEST
- Hard's reaction speed, plus openly-declared advantages: its units train 50%
  faster, and every technology its civilisation can hold is researched free
  and instantly the moment it enters each era - including the upgrades that
  convert its standing army. It still has to earn each age.

NOTIFICATIONS
- "You are under attack!" - with a warning siren and a marker on the minimap
  showing where, so a raid on an outlying farm no longer reads the same as one
  on your town centre.
- The chat feed now announces every player's era advance by name, so you can
  see an opponent hit the War era before their tanks arrive.

EVERYTHING FROM 2.15.0
- Aircraft carriers, with naval and amphibious AI to use them.
- Ballistic missile launchers: pack to move, deploy to fire, 420mm blast.
- Royal Marines, radar, and an expanded naval roster.
- Post-game statistics screen: timeline, economy, largest battle.
- Victory/defeat banner and the nuke cinematic.
- Starting-age selection, plane garrisoning, and an objective HUD.
- Three-row command card, AoE-style shift-queueing, and per-item rebindable
  hotkeys with a Classic AoE2 preset.
- Germany campaign expanded to five levels; campaign age caps, population
  caps, on-tile triggers and dormant independence.
- Fixed mid-battle freezes (per-step rally pathfinding budget and a tighter
  frame catch-up cap).
- Villagers no longer get stuck: every movement path is routed and watched.
